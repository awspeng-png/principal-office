A Message from the Principal

In Situn, I learn by seeing — the children, the teachers, and myself.

Dear parents, teachers, and children,

Situn Elementary has been on this road since 1903 — more than 120 years of walking with generation after generation of children as they set out from here. I often say that learning is one adventure after another: there is a starting point, there are challenges, there are turns — and every single step counts.

"See & Turn" is the spirit of our school. First we see — noticing each child's spark and how the world is changing. Then we turn — turning what we see into action, letting thinking carry our children forward. Whether it is reading, technology, aesthetics, or our local-culture lessons in the old streets of Situn, we hope every child walks with artful eyes and exploring feet, drawing a learning path that is entirely their own.

You are always welcome on campus — come adventure with us, alongside the children.

Alex Peng, PrincipalTAICHUNG SITUN ELEMENTARY SCHOOL

Our Vision

Four directions that shape how we adventure alongside our children.

LOCAL｜Roots

Endemic to SitunLocal identity is where the adventure begins

Children who grow up in Situn are an “endemic species” of this land (西屯特有種) — one of a kind. Setting out from Xidadun Old Street and walking into Liemei Hall and the Zhang Liao Family Temple, every grade has its own walk-and-learn course, so children get to know their hometown on foot and grow a sense of belonging — to themselves and to this place.

  • Identity
  • Diversity & co-flourishing
  • Sustainability
METHOD｜How

See & TurnFrom seeing to doing

First we see, then we turn. We notice each child's spark and how the world is changing, and then turn what we see into action. It is the spirit of Situn — and the way we think through every decision at school.

  • Think
  • Transform
  • Become Situn
HEART｜Care

SEL · Social and Emotional LearningSettle the heart first, then learn

Knowing your own emotions, understanding how others feel, and working well with people are skills that last a lifetime. Situn is working toward becoming an SEL-oriented school, so that children grow the courage to face each challenge within relationships where they feel safe.

  • Grounded in counseling & educational psychology
WORLD｜Wings

International EducationCarrying Situn's story out to befriend the world

Through video exchanges with partners in Japan, Korea, and Australia, children widen their horizons in real conversations. Deep roots let wings fly far — local identity and a global outlook are two ends of the same road.

  • Japan
  • Korea
  • Australia
