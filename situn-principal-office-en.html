A Message from the Principal

Dear parents, teachers, and children,

Situn Elementary has been on this road since 1903 — more than 120 years of walking with generation after generation of children as they set out from here. I often say that learning is one adventure after another: there is a starting point, there are challenges, there are turns — and every single step counts.

"See & Turn" is the spirit of our school. First we see — noticing each child's spark and how the world is changing. Then we turn — turning what we see into action, letting thinking carry our children forward. Whether it is reading, technology, aesthetics, or our local-culture lessons in the old streets of Situn, we hope every child walks with artful eyes and exploring feet, drawing a learning path that is entirely their own.

You are always welcome on campus — come adventure with us, alongside the children.

Alex Peng, PrincipalTAICHUNG SITUN ELEMENTARY SCHOOL
